Game data
---------
EXP table and map names: MeowDB / NiaDB (https://meowdb.com/msclassic), fan-run.
No Nexon game images ship with this program. The setup window cuts the few small
reference pictures it needs (minimap button, minimap header, your name tag) from your own
screen, and they stay on your PC.
