Maple Classic Companion (test build)
====================================

A leveling tracker for MapleStory Classic World: EXP per hour, time to the next level,
best maps, and a diary of your milestones.

It only looks at the game window, like screen recording. It never reads the game's memory,
never touches its network traffic, and never presses keys for you.

Start it
--------
1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere you like (for example your Documents folder).
   Keep the files together; the program needs the "_internal" folder next to it.
2. Double-click MapleClassicCompanion.exe.
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because this test build isn't signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. A setup window opens. Have MapleStory running in windowed mode, logged in, with the
   minimap open in the top-left corner. Follow the steps (about a minute).

After setup, a small window shows your level and EXP per hour, and an icon sits in the tray
near the clock. Right-click the small window for Settings, the journal, and "Run setup…".

To stop it: right-click the small window > "Exit and stop tracker".

Updates
-------
On first start the app copies itself to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\MapleClassicCompanion and runs
from there, so you can delete this unzipped folder afterwards (starting it again just opens the
newest version). New versions download by themselves in the background and switch over when
you're not playing (or right away: tray icon > "Restart to update"). Your data is kept.
Turn it off in Settings > Internet (one switch for all internet use, and one per feature).

Your data (stats, diary, settings) is saved in:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\MapleClassicCompanion
Delete that folder to start over. To uninstall, also delete
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\MapleClassicCompanion
