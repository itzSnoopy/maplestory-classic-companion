Custom artwork for the helper.

icon.png  - the app icon: original round artwork from the player's artist friend (2026-10-28,
            512x512, transparent corners). Used for the tray icon and helper preview (the tray draws a
            state-colored ring on its edge), the exe/shortcut icon (packaging/build.py -> icon.ico) and
            the setup window. It ships in the release zip. Restart the helper after changing it.
            Without it, the built-in placeholder (arrow + EXP bar) is used.

discord_icon.png (project root) - the same art, for the Discord app's `icon` art asset (upload by
            hand in the Discord developer portal). Refresh with: copy art/icon.png over it.

No AI-generated art, no Nexon art/sprites here (root rule 1).
